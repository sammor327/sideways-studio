                                                    Each picture shows the preview data as it stands. Click one to put that graphic in preview, click it again to take it out. Its options appear under Graphic features. The star beside a name keeps that graphic in Favorites; the chain copies that graphic's browser-source link for OBS or vMix.

                                                    Showdown Overlay

                                                    The cards played onto a showdown, in play order, the newest lifted because it resolves first. Airs only while a showdown is open: Open under Match data › Hands and showdown, which also switches this graphic on in preview.

                                                    Game intro Overlay

                                                    Round and game number over both players' legend, champion and battlefield, built in over the game window of the in-game overlay that is up.

                                                    Sideboard fly-in Overlay

                                                    Both players' sideboards at once, player 1 across the top and player 2 across the bottom, each flying in from their side (or one player alone, under Graphic features).

                                                    Odds to draw Overlay

                                                    The cards a player's deck can still give them, the likeliest first, on their side of the game window (or both players'). From their list under Match data › Decks and battlefields less the cards seen this game, or counted by a RiftAtlas game.

                                                    Trash Overlay

                                                    A player's trash (Riftbound's graveyard), the newest first, cards with Flow lit up with their Flow cost. Fill it under Match data › Trash and draw odds; a RiftAtlas game fills it on its own.

                                                    Sideboard card spotted Overlay

                                                    A card a player sided in, flown in over the game with "Sideboard Card for" and their name when it turns up in their hand after turn 1. Once it is on air it waits and flies each card in as it is spotted: Live game spots them from RiftAtlas, or spot one by hand under Graphic features.

                                                    Sponsor plate Overlay

                                                    A 3:1 plate that rotates through your sponsors and docks into whichever in-game overlay is up, in that overlay's colours. Add sponsors under Graphic features › Sponsor plate; with none added, a click here opens that list.

                                                    Card popup Overlay

                                                    One card flies in over the feed, or docks into the rows, dual-column or portrait overlay when one is up. Search it under Graphic features › Card popup; with nothing staged, a click here opens that search.

                                                    Card row Full frame

                                                    One to four cards side by side over the look's ground (an overlay with Background off). Pick them under Graphic features › Card row; with nothing picked, a click here opens the slots.

                                                    Legend distribution Full frame

                                                    The most played legends: a pie beside a table of each legend's share and win rate. Fill it under Match data › Legend distribution, or load it from the Tournament platform tab.

                                                    Matchup matrix Full frame

                                                    Every legend's win rate against every other: the most played legends across and down, each cell coloured by who is ahead. Paste matchups or load a Rift Registry event under Match data › Matchup matrix, or load the event from the Tournament platform tab.

                                                    Pairings Full frame

                                                    Every table of the round, 32 a page in two columns, with each finished table's result. Load a round from the Tournament platform tab, or type the tables under Match data › Pairings.

                                                    Ongoing matches Full frame

                                                    The pairings' tables still being played, bigger the fewer are left. Loaded from the Tournament platform tab, finished tables leave it on their own.

                                                    Results ticker Overlay

                                                    The round's tables along the bottom of the screen, a page at a time: each player's legend icon and name and the result, rolling on to the next tables every few seconds. With an in-game overlay up it fits along the bottom of the game area. Results come in from the Tournament platform tab as tables finish.

                                                              Match card

                                                              Who chose first, season records and team tags live under Match data › Match card and result and Player details.

                                                              Head to head, VS

                                                              Player names, legends, the round and the event name come from Match data; each card is that player's legend card. The logo between the cards is the one uploaded under Look and setup › Font and logo; its ground is the glowing arrows, changeable under Look.

                                                              Player profile

                                                              Team, store, season record and top finishes are under Match data › Player details; the decklist is the player's list under Decks and battlefields (with it on, the top finishes make room for it). The camera well bottom right is transparent for a player feed behind this source.

                                                              Bracket

                                                              Players, format and results are under Match data › Bracket. Click a match's winner there; the bracket on air follows the next TAKE.

                                                              Standings

                                                              Twenty rows a page, one group at a time: › runs on from a group's last page into the next group. Rows and the cut are under Match data › Standings; the Tournament platform tab loads every group at once.
                                                              Highlight

                                                                A highlighted player's row lights up and grows while the rest dim, on air at once with no TAKE, and the graphic turns to their group and page. Up to eight at a time; × takes one off.

                                                                Pairings

                                                                32 tables a page. The tables are under Match data › Pairings; the Tournament platform tab loads a whole round (or one group) at once, and loading it again brings in new results.
                                                                Highlight

                                                                  A highlighted table grows in its column while the rest dim, on air at once with no TAKE, and the graphic turns to its page. It stays on its table as results come in. Up to eight at a time; × takes one off.

                                                                  Ongoing matches

                                                                  Draws the tables under Match data › Pairings that have no result yet: up to 12 in one column, bigger the fewer there are, then two columns, 32 a page.

                                                                  Results ticker

                                                                  Legend distribution

                                                                  The legends, the player count, the label and the win rate note are under Match data › Legend distribution.
                                                                  Table roll
                                                                  Highlight

                                                                  A table longer than nine rows rolls; the roll and the highlight act on air at once, no TAKE needed. A highlighted legend's slice comes out of the pie and grows, its row lights up, and a rolling table holds with that row in view until the highlight comes off.

                                                                  Matchup matrix

                                                                  On the grid
                                                                  Highlight

                                                                  Click a legend at the start of a row to light its row, one at the top to light its column, or a cell to lift that matchup (the diagonal lights a legend's row and column). The highlight acts on air at once, no TAKE needed; the legends and the settings above wait for TAKE.

                                                                  Result

                                                                  The winner and the "advances to" line are under Match data › Match card and result. Unset, the strip names whoever holds the series.

                                                                  Game intro

                                                                  The round is Event › Round title. Each side shows its legend, champion and Battlefield from Match data. It fits itself into whichever in-game overlay is up; TAKE plays the build, and taking it off fades it out.

                                                                  Sideboard fly-in

                                                                  From each player's list under Match data › Decks and battlefields: player 1 flies in from the left across the top of the game, player 2 from the right across the bottom. A player whose list has no sideboard is left out.

                                                                  Sideboard card spotted

                                                                    Spots act on air at once, like the clock: TAKE this graphic once and it flies each card in as it is spotted, then out again. Live game (Match data) spots the cards a player sided in from game 2 on, when they turn up in hand after turn 1.

                                                                    Odds to draw

                                                                    Each chance is the card's copies still in the deck over the cards not yet seen, likeliest first, a bar under each as long as its chance against the likeliest's. The deck is the player's list under Match data › Decks and battlefields less the cards seen this game (Trash and draw odds), or a RiftAtlas game's own count. It sits on the player's side of the game window of whichever in-game overlay is up.

                                                                    Trash

                                                                    The cards under Match data › Trash and draw odds, the newest first. A card with Flow lights up with its Flow cost, and the sheet's last line says what Flow does. A trash longer than the sheet scrolls through, the way the hand lists do.

                                                                    Decklists side by side

                                                                    Each half reads that player's list under Match data › Decks and battlefields.
